## Which sizes to prioritise

If you post the same visual everywhere, three shapes cover almost everything:

1. **Vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920)** for Stories, Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
2. **Portrait 4:5 (1080 × 1350)** for Instagram and Facebook feeds, where it takes up the most screen space.
3. **Landscape 1.91:1 (1200 × 630)** for LinkedIn, Facebook links, X and your website's share image.

## Tips that save you from bad crops

- **Keep text in the middle.** Each platform crops slightly differently, and some overlay buttons or captions near the edges, especially on Stories, Reels and TikTok, where the top and bottom 15 to 20% are often covered.
- **Start from the biggest, highest-quality image you have.** Scaling down looks sharp; scaling up looks blurry.
- **Check the profile grid on Instagram.** Since 2025 the grid shows posts as 3:4 rectangles, so a 4:5 post gets slightly trimmed at the top and bottom in the grid view.
- **Banners are seen at very different widths.** LinkedIn and YouTube banners get cropped heavily on phones. Keep logos and text in a central safe area.
- **Export at the exact size.** Uploading a 6000-pixel photo lets the platform compress it its own way, often badly. Upload the size it wants.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best image size for social media in 2026?

There isn't one size for all. Use 1080 × 1350 for Instagram and Facebook feeds, 1080 × 1920 for Stories, Reels and TikTok, and 1200 × 627 to 1200 × 630 for LinkedIn and link previews.

### What size should a LinkedIn image be?

1200 × 627 pixels for feed images and link previews, 1200 × 1200 for square posts, and 1584 × 396 for your profile banner.

### What is the YouTube thumbnail size?

1280 × 720 pixels, a 16:9 ratio. Keep text large, since thumbnails are often seen very small.
