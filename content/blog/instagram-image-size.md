Instagram rewards images that fill the screen and punishes ones that don't fit: wrong shapes get cropped, small files get blurry, and text near the edges gets hidden under buttons. Here are the sizes to use in 2026.

## Instagram image sizes at a glance

| Format | Size (pixels) | Ratio | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait post | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | Most feed posts, takes the most screen space |
| 3:4 grid post | 1080 × 1440 | 3:4 | Matches the profile grid exactly |
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | Classic look, carousels |
| Landscape post | 1080 × 566 | 1.91:1 | Rarely worth it, appears small |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Full-screen Stories |
| Reel | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Full-screen video and its cover |
| Profile picture | 320 × 320 | 1:1 | Shown as a circle |

## Portrait 4:5 or the 3:4 grid?

Since early 2025, Instagram's profile grid shows posts as tall 3:4 rectangles instead of squares. That leaves you two good options:

- **4:5 (1080 × 1350)** is still the tallest shape that shows uncropped in the feed, so it takes the most space when people scroll. In the 3:4 grid view, a thin slice is trimmed from the top and bottom.
- **3:4 (1080 × 1440)** matches the grid exactly, so your profile looks precisely as designed.

If your profile grid is part of your brand, design for 3:4. If feed reach matters more, 4:5 is the safe pick. Either way, keep important content away from the very top and bottom.

## Stories and Reels: mind the safe zone

Stories and Reels are 1080 × 1920, but the interface covers parts of it: your profile name at the top, and captions, buttons and the reply bar at the bottom. Keep text and faces out of roughly the top 250 and bottom 340 pixels.

## Why your Instagram photos look blurry

- **Uploading too small.** Anything under 1080 pixels wide gets upscaled and looks soft.
- **Uploading way too big.** Instagram compresses huge files aggressively. Export at exactly 1080 pixels wide.
- **Uploading over a weak connection.** Instagram may use lower quality on slow mobile data. Upload on wifi where possible.
- **Heavy text and fine detail.** Compression struggles with tiny text. Keep text large.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Instagram post size in 2026?

1080 × 1350 (4:5) for the most space in the feed, or 1080 × 1440 (3:4) if you want posts to match the profile grid exactly.

### What size is an Instagram Story?

1080 × 1920 pixels, a 9:16 ratio. Keep text away from the top and bottom, where Instagram's buttons sit.

### Why does Instagram crop my photo?

Instagram only accepts ratios between 1.91:1 (wide) and 4:5 (tall) for feed posts, and the profile grid shows 3:4. Resize to one of the recommended sizes before uploading.

### What resolution should I upload to Instagram?

1080 pixels wide. Instagram scales everything to that width, so larger files just get compressed more.
